# Inkfold

**Private mockup studio for print-on-demand sellers.**
Put your designs on your own mockup photos at true print size, keep necklaces and straps *in front* of the print, and export ready-to-post images for Etsy, Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.

👉 **Use it here:** https://inkfoldstudio.github.io

- 🔒 Private – your designs and mockups stay on your computer.
- 📐 True print size · 👕 Fabric wrap · 📿 Keep-in-front brush
- 🗂 Library with categories · 📤 Exports for every platform · ☁️ Folder sync (Chrome & Edge)

Works best in Chrome or Edge. Click **Install** in the address bar to use it like a normal app.
